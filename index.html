About Me

Academic Profile

I am Dr. Mahadev Mallikarjun Biradar, Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Basaveshwar Engineering College (Autonomous), Bagalkote, Karnataka.

My academic and research interests include fluid dynamics, magnetohydrodynamics, nanofluids, heat and mass transfer, mathematical modelling and related areas of applied mathematics.

I am actively involved in teaching, research, academic administration, research guidance and various academic activities. My research work focuses on mathematical modelling and analytical and numerical investigation of transport phenomena in complex fluid systems.

I have published over 25 research papers in reputed international journals, authored books and book chapters, and filed multiple patents. I have served as Head of the Department, Chairman of the Board of Studies, and held various administrative roles.

Research Interests

Areas of Academic and Research Interest

Magnetohydrodynamics

Mathematical modelling of MHD flows and transport phenomena under the influence of magnetic fields.

Nanofluids

Research on hybrid and ternary hybrid nanofluids and their thermal and transport characteristics.

Fluid Dynamics

Analytical and numerical studies of complex fluid flow models and transport phenomena.

Heat Transfer

Mass Transfer
