👨‍🏫 About Me

Academic Profile

Welcome to my official academic website. I am Dr. Mahadev Mallikarjun Biradar, currently serving as an Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Basaveshwar Engineering College, Bagalkote, Karnataka, India. With over a decade of experience in teaching and research, I am deeply passionate about making mathematics accessible, engaging, and applicable to real-world problems.

My primary research interests lie in the field of Mathematical Modelling, where I explore the application of differential equations, numerical methods, and computational techniques to solve complex problems in engineering, physics, and biological systems. I believe that mathematical modelling is not just an abstract discipline but a powerful tool that bridges the gap between theoretical mathematics and practical innovation.

Throughout my academic career, I have published numerous research papers in reputed national and international journals, authored books on mathematics, and actively contributed to curriculum development at the undergraduate and postgraduate levels. I am also committed to mentoring young researchers and guiding students in their academic pursuits through project work and collaborative research.

On this website, you will find detailed information about my educational background, teaching philosophy, research publications, books, patents, awards, and administrative contributions. I regularly update this platform with resources for students, including syllabus materials, notification links, and free learning tools. Feel free to explore the pages and connect with me through the contact section or my professional profiles on Google Scholar, ResearchGate, and LinkedIn.

Thank you for visiting, and I hope you find this site informative and useful for your academic and professional needs.

🔬 Research Interests

Core Research Areas

Magnetohydrodynamics

Mathematical modelling of MHD flows and transport phenomena under magnetic fields.

Nanofluids

Research on hybrid and ternary hybrid nanofluids and their thermal characteristics.

Fluid Dynamics

Analytical and numerical studies of complex fluid flow models.

Heat Transfer

Thermal radiation, heat generation and related transport phenomena.

Mass Transfer

Studies involving concentration fields, chemical reaction and mass transfer.

Mathematical Modelling

Application of mathematical techniques to engineering and physical problems.

⚡ Current Research

Anomalous Diffusion in Ternary Nanofluids

Exploring Maxwell models with viscous dissipation and Nield boundary bioconvection.

MHD Prandtl Fluid Flow

Perturbation analysis with thermal radiation and double diffusion effects.

Casson Nanoliquid Optimization

Using Taguchi technology for radiative-magnetic-chemical reactive effects.
