👨‍🏫 About Me

Academic Profile

I am Dr. Mahadev Mallikarjun Biradar, Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Basaveshwar Engineering College (Autonomous), Bagalkote, Karnataka.

My academic and research interests include fluid dynamics, magnetohydrodynamics, nanofluids, heat and mass transfer, mathematical modelling and related areas of applied mathematics.

I am actively involved in teaching, research, academic administration, research guidance and various academic activities. My research work focuses on mathematical modelling and analytical and numerical investigation of transport phenomena in complex fluid systems.

I have published over 26 research papers in reputed international journals, authored books and book chapters, and filed multiple patents. I have served as Head of the Department, Chairman of the Board of Studies, and held various administrative roles.

🔬 Research Interests

Core Research Areas

Magnetohydrodynamics

Mathematical modelling of MHD flows and transport phenomena under magnetic fields.

Nanofluids

Research on hybrid and ternary hybrid nanofluids and their thermal characteristics.

Fluid Dynamics

Analytical and numerical studies of complex fluid flow models.

Heat Transfer

Thermal radiation, heat generation and related transport phenomena.

Mass Transfer

Studies involving concentration fields, chemical reaction and mass transfer.

Mathematical Modelling

Application of mathematical techniques to engineering and physical problems.

⚡ Current Research

Anomalous Diffusion in Ternary Nanofluids

Exploring Maxwell models with viscous dissipation and Nield boundary bioconvection.

MHD Prandtl Fluid Flow

Perturbation analysis with thermal radiation and double diffusion effects.

Casson Nanoliquid Optimization

Using Taguchi technology for radiative-magnetic-chemical reactive effects.
