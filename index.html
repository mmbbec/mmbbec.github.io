About Me

Academic Profile

I am Dr. Mahadev Mallikarjun Biradar, an Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Basaveshwar Engineering College (Autonomous), Bagalkote, Karnataka.

My academic and research interests include fluid dynamics, magnetohydrodynamics, nanofluids, heat and mass transfer, mathematical modelling and related areas of applied mathematics.

I am actively involved in teaching, research, academic administration, research guidance and various academic activities.

This website presents my academic profile, research work, publications, books, patents and professional activities.
