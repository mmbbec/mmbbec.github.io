ℹ️ VTU CBCS Grading System

VTU SGPA Formula

SGPA = Sum(Credits × Grade Points) / Sum(Credits)

VTU Percentage Formula (New)

Percentage = (CGPA - 0.5) × 10 (w.e.f 2021 scheme, for 2018 it's CGPA×10)

Grade Points

O=10, A+=9, A=8, B+=7, B=6, C=5, P=4, F=0
