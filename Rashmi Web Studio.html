FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost?

Website cost depends on the number of pages, design requirements, and functionality. Contact us with your requirements and we'll provide a detailed quote.

How long does it take to build a website?

Simple websites can generally be completed within a few days. Larger, more complex projects may take 2–4 weeks depending on the scope.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. All websites are designed to be fully responsive across phones, tablets, and desktops.

Do you provide domain and hosting assistance?

Yes. We provide assistance with domain registration and hosting setup to get your website online.

Can I update my website later?

Yes. Content updates and maintenance services are available. We can also provide training if needed.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Absolutely. We can modernize and redesign your existing website while preserving your brand identity.
