📋 Google Sheets Setup Required

This app uses Google Sheets as a shared database. All devices will sync automatically.

  1. Create a Google Sheet with 4 tabs: Subjects, Users, Attendance, Announcements
  2. Share the sheet with "Anyone with the link can edit"
  3. Get the Sheet ID from the URL: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/YOUR_SHEET_ID/edit
  4. Paste the Sheet ID below
